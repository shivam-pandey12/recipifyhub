Privacy notice

Privacy, handled with clarity.

RecipifyHub uses the minimum practical data needed to run your account, planner, saved recipes, and session experience. Powered by MH Horizon.

What we store

Account details you provide such as username, email, password hash, profile settings, meal plans, saved recipes, comments, ratings, and usage history that supports your experience.

Why we store it

To keep you signed in, personalize your profile, save meal plans, support recipe interactions, and improve product reliability across the RecipifyHub workflow.

Browser storage

Your browser may store theme preference, newsletter form state, redirect helpers, and cookie-consent preference. Essential session cookies are used for authentication.

How long data stays

Account and content data stay available while your account remains active unless you request deletion or remove data through the app.

Contact

For privacy questions related to RecipifyHub and MH Horizon, use the project contact channel you manage for this deployment.
